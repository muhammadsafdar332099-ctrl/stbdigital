Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Welcome to STB DIGITAL. We respect the privacy of visitors to our official website at stbdigital.site. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website or contact us, how it may be used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Scope: This policy applies to the STB DIGITAL business website only. Any mobile game or application published by STB DIGITAL may have different data practices and should have its own privacy notice covering the SDKs, advertising, analytics, and other services actually used by that app.

1. Who We Are

STB DIGITAL is a digital business focused on mobile games and digital experiences. For questions about this Privacy Policy or information you have provided to us, email contact@stbdigital.site.

2. Information We May Receive

Information you voluntarily provide: If you email us, we may receive your email address, name (if included), the contents of your message, attachments, and any other details you choose to share.

Technical information: When you browse the website, our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, and infrastructure providers involved in delivering the site may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. We do not directly operate or control all of these provider logs.

No account registration: This informational website does not currently offer visitor accounts, purchases, or an on-site registration form.

3. How Information Is Used

We may use information to answer questions and business inquiries, provide support, maintain correspondence, protect the website from abuse, troubleshoot technical problems, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use information sent to our contact email to sell personal profiles.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

At the time this policy was published, STB DIGITAL has not intentionally installed advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, or marketing cookies in this static website. Essential technical functions operated by browsers, hosting providers, or linked external services may still involve cookies or similar technologies under those providers' policies.

If we add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technology in the future, we will review and update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

5. Hosting and Service Providers

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process limited technical data to host, secure, and deliver website content. If you contact us by email, your email provider and our business email provider may process correspondence to deliver and store messages. Their handling of information is subject to their applicable terms and privacy notices.

6. When We May Share Information

We do not sell personal information that you submit by email. We may disclose information to service providers as reasonably necessary to operate the website or manage correspondence; to comply with a valid legal request or applicable law; to protect rights, security, and prevent misuse; or as part of a lawful business reorganization where appropriate safeguards apply.

7. Data Retention

We keep email correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the message. Hosting-provider logs may be retained according to the provider's own policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control and intend to use HTTPS for website access. However, no online service or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive information by ordinary email.

9. International Processing

Website hosting and email services may involve processing or storage of information in countries other than your own. Where relevant, these services operate under their respective privacy and security practices and applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, or object to certain processing. To make a request, contact contact@stbdigital.site. We may need to verify a request before acting on it, and some information may need to be retained where legally required.

11. Children's Privacy

This business website is intended to provide general information about STB DIGITAL and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party services you choose to visit.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Please review this page periodically.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or privacy-related requests, contact STB DIGITAL at contact@stbdigital.site, or visit our Contact Us page.
